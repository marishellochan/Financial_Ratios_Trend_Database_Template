# Financial Ratios Trend Database Template

This Excel-based database template is designed to assist businesses in tracking financial ratios and their trends over time. Created with customizable macros, it adapts to the specific requirements of any business or company. The database provides an intuitive interface for inputting financial data, generating trends, and visualizing yearly averages through graphs.

## Key Features
- **Customizable Macros**: Tailor the database to suit your business requirements.
- **Financial Ratio Tracking**: Monitor key ratios on a quarterly basis.
- **Trend Analysis**: Automatically generate yearly trend sheets and visualizations.
- **Color-Coded Indicators**: Quickly identify whether financial targets are met (green for success, red for unmet targets).
- **Data Reset Option**: Easily reset the template for reuse or significant changes.

## How to Use the Financial Ratios Database Template

### 1. **Read Instructions**
Begin by familiarizing yourself with these steps to ensure effective use of the template.

### 2. **Fill Out the Form Sheet**
Navigate to the sheet labeled **"Form"** and provide the following details:
- Business name
- Number of ratios to track
- Number of financial statements (up to 5 ratios)
- Names and equations for each ratio

### 3. **Create the Database**
Once the form is complete:
- Click the **"Create"** button to generate the database.

### 4. **Input Quarterly Data**
Go to the **"F.Ratios Data"** sheet and input data for each quarter (March, June, September, December) for one year at a time:
- Data that meets the required thresholds will turn **green**.
- Data that falls short of requirements will turn **red**.

### 5. **Generate a New Year Trend Sheet**
After entering a full year of data:
- Click the **"Create New Year Trend"** button to generate a trend sheet for the most recent year.

### 6. **Update Yearly Trends**
Once the **"Create New Year Trend"** button is clicked:
- The **"F.Ratios Yearly Trends"** sheet will automatically update with yearly averages and trends displayed in graphs.

### 7. **Make Corrections**
If you need to correct data:
- **For the last year added**:
  1. Delete the trend sheet for that year.
  2. Remove the corresponding data from the **"F.Ratios Yearly Trends"** sheet (Year and Average columns).
  3. Update the data in the **"F.Ratios Data"** sheet.
  4. Click **"Create New Year Trend"** to regenerate and update the database.
- **For earlier years**:
  - Reset the database and start over (see Step 8).
  - **Tip**: Double-check all data before proceeding to subsequent years to avoid unnecessary corrections.

### 8. **Reset the Database**
To start fresh or apply significant changes:
- Click the **"Reset"** button on the **"Form"** sheet to clear all data.

### 9. **Final Tips**
- Avoid altering template sheets or merging cells to ensure the macros function correctly.
- If needed, start with a fresh template file instead of resetting the current one.
